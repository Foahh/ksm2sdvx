Bundled audio dependencies and assets

K-Shoot MANIA v2: https://github.com/kshootmania/ksm-v2
ksmaudio: https://github.com/kshootmania/ksmaudio
BASS and BASS FX: https://www.un4seen.com/

The five KSM preset WAV samples are excluded from KSM's MIT license.
Local builds include these assets from the pinned upstream checkout. Public
redistribution requires separate permission from the relevant rights holders.
Their presence in the upstream repository does not grant an MIT license to them.

BASS is free for non-commercial use under its own terms; commercial use requires
the appropriate license. BASS FX and its dependencies retain their own license
and attribution requirements. The installed renderer includes the upstream
third-party notices in its licenses directory.

KSM's source license does not replace the licenses of nested dependencies.
Establish ksmaudio source redistribution rights before publishing binaries.
